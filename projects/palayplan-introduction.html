Helping farmers plan smarter with PalayPlan

PalayPlan is a mobile application concept created to support rice farmers in organizing field activities, planning schedules, and making more informed decisions through a simple and user-friendly digital experience.

Flutter Mobile App Agriculture Planning System

What the app is for

  • Helps farmers organize planting and harvesting schedules
  • Allows better tracking of farm activities and tasks
  • Provides a more readable and structured workflow for decision-making
  • Supports planning through a simple mobile interface
The goal of PalayPlan is to simplify agricultural planning with technology, making important farming tasks easier to manage and more efficient for users.

Key purpose

This project focuses on making farm planning more organized and efficient. It is designed to give users a cleaner way to manage important agricultural activities and improve overall productivity through a mobile-first approach.
